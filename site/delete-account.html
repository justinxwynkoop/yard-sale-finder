Delete instantly in the app

The fastest way — deletion is immediate and permanent:

  1. Open Trove and go to the Profile tab
  2. Tap Account
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account
  4. Confirm — your account and data are removed right away

Or request deletion here

If you no longer have the app installed (or can't sign in), email us from the address associated with your account and we'll delete it within a few business days:

Request account deletion

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your profile (name, city, avatar, preferences)
  • Every sale and listing you posted, including photos
  • Your messages, saved items, follows, and notification tokens

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We don't keep your personal data after deletion; anonymous, aggregate records (for example abuse-report tallies required for safety and legal compliance) may be retained without any link to your identity.
